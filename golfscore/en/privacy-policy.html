Privacy Policy — Golfscore

Last updated: 2026-10-28

Golfscore does not collect, transmit or share any personal data. It has no account, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no network communication.

Where your data lives

The score card you scan (players' names and handicaps, course, pars) and the scores you enter are stored only on your device, inside the app. We never receive them. Deleting a card, or the app, deletes them from your phone.

Sharing the results

The scores leave your phone only when you show the results QR code to the organiser, who scans it. Nothing is sent over the internet.

Camera and photos

The camera is used only to read QR codes. The image is analysed on your device and is neither stored nor sent. If you pick a photo of a QR code from your library, only that photo is read, with your permission.

Information about other people

The names and handicaps shown come from the card prepared by the organiser, who is responsible for informing the players. Share the scores only with the organiser.

Third parties

Golfscore contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking component.

Children

Golfscore is not directed at children and collects no data about anyone.

Your rights

Since we hold none of your data, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. You can delete everything yourself by deleting the cards or the app.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above is updated. The latest version is always on this page.
