1 What is Golforg?

Golforg helps an organiser run golf tournaments of one or several days. You enter your players and courses, set the schedule, draw the groups, print score cards, then enter the scores — with buttons, or by scanning the pencil-filled cards. Rankings update by themselves and can be exported as a PDF.

Everything stays on your iPhone. There is no account, no server and no internet connection needed on the course. The flip side: you are responsible for your backups (see “Backup and restore”).

  • Stroke play: gross and net rankings, plus a putts ranking.
  • Stableford: points per hole, net and gross.
  • Shamble and Scramble: team formats, ranked by group and day, with an overall ranking.

2 Quick start

  1. Add your players (Home → Players).
  2. Add your courses with pars and indexes (Home → Courses).
  3. Create a tournament with the + button.
  4. Register the players and set the schedule: a course and a format for each day.
  5. Draw the groups and print the score cards.
  6. Enter the scores with the buttons, or scan the cards.
  7. Read the rankings and export them as a PDF.
  8. Back up your data (Home → Backup).

3 Players

Home → Players → +. A player has a first name, a last name and a handicap from 0 to 54 (decimals allowed). Names are shown as “First LAST”.

To enter many players at once, use Import a list: one line per player, First LAST ; handicap, for example John SMITH ; 13 or John ; Smith ; 13. A player who already exists (same name) just gets the new handicap.

4 Courses

Home → Courses → +. Give the course a name (and a country if you like), then set for each of the 18 holes its par (3 to 5) and its index (1 to 18, each used once).

The index tells on which holes handicap strokes are received. It is used for net scores and for Stableford. The app warns you if an index is used twice.

5 Create a tournament

Home → +. Enter a name and the number of days (1 to 14). You can set the first day: the days are then consecutive. A rest day can be added afterwards.

The tournament sheet gathers everything: Players, Schedule, Tournament rules, Draw, the Days, the Rankings and the PDF export. At the bottom, “Erase all scores” (handy after a trial run) and “Delete this tournament” both ask you to copy a code.

6 Players and schedule

Players: tick the players who take part. Schedule: for each day, add a course and a format (Stroke play, Stableford, Shamble or Scramble). The same course can come back another day with another format.

  • Schedule a course over several days sets the same course and format on the days you choose in one go.
  • Add a course or a rest day: a rest day is a day without golf.
  • Insert a rest day before this day adds a day to the stay; the following days are renumbered and shifted, with their groups and scores.
  • Change the date… moves a day; with “Also shift the following days”, the rest of the stay moves too.
Scheduling a Stableford day creates the Stableford rankings by itself. For the other formats, use “Generate from the formats” in Rankings by format.

7 Tournament rules

  • Picked-up ball: a player who picks up scores par + N on the hole in gross and net (5 by default), 0 points in Stableford. In team formats (shamble, scramble) a picked-up score only counts if the others have none.
  • Count putts: when on, entering a hole asks for strokes and putts, and the Putts ranking is available. When off, only strokes are asked.

8 Formats and scoring

  • Net = strokes minus the strokes received. A player receives their handicap (rounded) spread over the holes: a handicap of 20 gives 1 stroke on every hole plus a second one on the 2 holes with the lowest indexes (1 and 2).
  • Stableford (against par, net or gross): par 2 points, bogey 1, double bogey or worse 0, birdie 3, eagle 4, albatross 5. A picked-up ball scores 0.
  • Shamble: for each hole the group's best net counts. Scramble: the group's best gross counts. Rankings are given per group and per day, and overall per player.
  • Ranks: equal totals share a rank (1, 1, 3). A partial total (holes not all played) can only be compared at an equal number of holes.

9 Draw the groups

Tournament → Draw the groups. Choose the days (or all days at once) and the number of players per group (2 to 6), then Propose a draw. The Check section lists the constraints that are not met. Tap a player, then another one on the same day, to swap them. Choose a course for each day, then Validate and create the groups.

Draw constraints guide the draw: do not replay with the same partners, balance handicaps, separate or keep together some players, fix a player in a group, mark a player as unavailable. Each one is strict or a preference with a weight from 1 to 10.

Redo or cancel an existing draw: the app shows a number you must copy (valid 5 minutes). It is not possible once scores exist: erase them first.

10 Score cards and entering scores

In the Draw screen, Score cards to print (PDF) produces one A4 landscape page per group, with the course, the players and a QR code. Print at 100 % (not “fit to page”) and keep the 4 black squares in the corners clear.

Players fill in the card in pencil, one digit per box, legibly. For a picked-up ball, write R in the strokes box.

With the buttons: Day → group. The holes are shown on top; use − and + for strokes and putts, switch on “Picked up” if needed, then Save hole. The saved line shows the net and the Stableford points. You can erase a hole for the group, or swipe a player to erase their score.

By scanning: Day → Scan a score card (or the scan button in a group). Photograph the whole page, flat and well lit, with the QR code and the 4 black squares visible. The app reads the digits on the phone. Cells it is unsure about appear in orange or red: correct them (you can show only the holes to check), then Save the scores.

The photo is processed on your iPhone and is neither stored nor sent anywhere. The app refuses a card that belongs to another tournament, day or group.

11 Rankings and PDF

Rankings Gross / Net / Putts add up all the days. Rankings by format (Stableford, shamble, scramble) can be limited to some courses, days or formats; use Generate from the formats to create the usual ones, or + to create your own (including a custom Stableford scale).

Export as PDF: tick the rankings you want, generate the PDF and share it (mail, messages, AirPrint…).

12 Backup and restore

Your data exists only on this phone. Back it up regularly: Home → Backup → Back up now, then choose where to save the file in the Files app (preferably iCloud Drive). A reminder appears on the home screen if you have not backed up for a week.

To restore (new phone, or after a problem): Backup → Restore a backup…, pick the file. The app shows what it contains and replaces all the data of the phone; the previous state is kept aside once, just in case.

13 Language, support and privacy

Home → Settings & help: choose the language (English, French, German, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Simplified Chinese, Russian; by default the phone's language), contact support (the mail includes technical details but never names), see the diagnostic log, open this guide or the privacy policy.

Golforg does not collect any data. Read the privacy policy.
