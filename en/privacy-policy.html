Privacy Policy — Golforg

Last updated: 2026-10-07

Golforg does not collect, transmit or share any personal data. It has no account, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no network communication for its features.

Where your data lives

The players (names, handicaps), courses, tournaments and scores you enter are stored only on your device, in a database inside the app. We never receive them. Depending on your iOS settings, the app's data may be included in your own iPhone or iCloud backups, which Apple manages.

Backups you create

The “Back up now” function creates a file that you save where you choose (Files, iCloud Drive…). You decide who can access it. Deleting the app deletes its data from the phone, not the backup files you saved.

Camera and photos

The camera is used only to scan score cards. The image is analysed on your device (QR code and handwritten digits) and is neither stored nor sent. If you pick a photo from your library, only that photo is read, with your permission.

Information about other people

Names and handicaps of the players are entered by you, the organiser. You are responsible for informing the players and, where the law requires it, for having their consent.

Support

If you contact support, your mail app sends the message only when you press send. It includes technical information: app version, device model, iOS version, languages, the number of players, courses, tournaments and scores, and how long ago you backed up. It never includes names. The diagnostic log, if you send it, contains technical events only (no names). We use this information only to answer you.

Third parties

Golforg contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking component.

Children

Golforg is not directed at children and collects no data about anyone.

Your rights

Since we hold none of your data, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. You can delete everything yourself at any time by deleting the tournaments and players in the app, or the app itself.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above is updated. The latest version is always on this page.

Support: golforg@icloud.com
